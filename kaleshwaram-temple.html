Kaleshwaram Temple Telangana – Sri Kaleshwara Mukteshwara Swamy Temple

Kaleshwaram Temple, dedicated to Lord Shiva, is one of the most revered Shiva temples of Telangana. Pilgrims come for darshan, abhishekam and a holy bath at the sacred Triveni Sangamam nearby. This guide from Rekshetra Tours covers the temple's significance, darshan, pujas and how to plan your visit.

Kaleshwaram Temple History

The Kaleshwaram Shiva Temple is one of the ancient Shaiva kshetras of the Telangana region, associated with the Godavari river and the Dakshina Kashi tradition. The temple's most distinctive feature is the pairing of two lingams on one peetham, worshipped as Kaleshwara (Shiva) and Mukteshwara (Yama). Devotees believe worship here grants relief from the fear of death and leads towards moksha.

Kaleshwaram Temple Darshan

Daily darshan, abhishekam and aarti are performed as per temple tradition. Mondays, Maha Shivaratri, Karthika Masam and festival days draw especially large crowds. Pilgrims commonly combine darshan with a Godavari snanam and a visit to the Triveni Sangamam.

Kaleshwaram Temple Timings

Temple timings can change on festival days and during special sevas. Please confirm the current darshan and puja timings with Rekshetra Tours or the temple office before travelling.

Pujas and Sevas at Kaleshwaram

  • Rudrabhishekam
  • Shaswata (permanent) Abhishekam, Shiva Kalyanam, Anna Puja and Nitya Nivedanam
  • Navagraha Puja, Rahu Ketu Puja and Kuja Dosha Puja
  • Annadanam seva

Triveni Sangamam and Nearby Places

The Godavari and Pranahita rivers meet near Kaleshwaram, and tradition holds that the Saraswati flows unseen, making this the Triveni Sangamam. Nearby pilgrimage and sightseeing spots in the region include Adi Mukteshwara, Sangameshwara, Mahadevpur temples, Manthani temples, Medigadda and Annaram barrages.
