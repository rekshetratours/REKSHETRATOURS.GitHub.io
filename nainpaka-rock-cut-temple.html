Nainpaka Rock-cut Temple Near Kaleshwaram

నైన్‌పాక రాతి ఆలయం

📍 Distance from Kaleshwaram temple: 22 km · 🏷️ Type: Heritage

Nainpaka is a rare rock-cut cave temple and one of the heritage highlights within the 50 km radius of Kaleshwaram, about 22 km from the main temple.

It suits visitors who want heritage sightseeing along with darshan, and works well in a two-day Kaleshwaram pilgrimage plan.

Why visit Nainpaka

Nainpaka is known as a rock-cut cave temple, an uncommon heritage stop in this region. Most visitors combine it with darshan at Kaleshwaram, since it is only about 22 km from the main temple and can be done in a half-day trip.

Plan your visit

  • Distance: about 22 km from Sri Kaleshwara Mukteshwara Swamy Temple. Check live directions before you leave, as road conditions change.
  • Time needed: allow around 1–2 hours at the site, plus travel time.
  • Best season: winter (November–February) is comfortable for sightseeing. See best time to visit Kaleshwaram.
  • Carry: drinking water, comfortable footwear and a cap. Dress modestly at temples.
  • Timings and access can change, especially during festivals. Please confirm locally.
