Mahadevpur Temples Near Kaleshwaram

మహాదేవ్‌పూర్ ఆలయాలు

📍 Distance from Kaleshwaram temple: 18 km · 🏷️ Type: Temple Group

Mahadevpur has a cluster of historic temples. Kaleshwaram itself falls under Mahadevpur Mandal of Jayashankar Bhupalpally district, which makes it a natural stop on a Kaleshwaram day trip.

The cluster is about 18 km from Kaleshwaram temple and can be combined with the Medigadda and Annaram barrages.
