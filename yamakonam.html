Yamakonam at Kaleshwaram Temple

యమకోణం

📍 Distance from Kaleshwaram temple: inside temple complex · 🏷️ Type: Temple Feature

Yamakonam is a triangular passage inside the Kaleshwaram temple premises. Devotees believe that those who bend and pass through it without hitting their head are spared the troubles of Yama, the god of death.

It is part of the temple complex, so it is covered during the normal darshan visit. The temple is also known for two Shiva lingas, Kaleshwara and Mukteshwara, on a single pedestal.
