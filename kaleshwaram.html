SRI KALESHWARA MUKTESHWARA SWAMY TEMPLE

Kaleshwaram Temple

A rare Shiva temple with two lingams — Kaleshwara and Mukteshwara — worshipped together, at the sacred confluence of the Godavari, Pranahitha and Saraswathi rivers.

About the Temple

Sri Kaleshwara Mukteshwara Swamy Temple stands at Kaleshwaram, on the banks of the Godavari, at its meeting point with the Pranahitha and the mythical, unseen Saraswathi — together known as the Triveni Sangamam. The temple is unusual for housing two Shiva lingams, Kaleshwara and Mukteshwara, sharing a single pedestal, worshipped together.

The site is closely tied to the region's temple-centred pilgrimage economy, and to the Ashta Theerthalu — the eight sacred bathing spots traditionally visited around Kaleshwaram as part of a complete yatra.

How to Reach

  • By Road: Kaleshwaram is well connected by road from Bhupalapally, Mancherial and Karimnagar.
  • By Rail: Nearest railway stations are at Ramagundam and Mancherial, with taxis available onward.
  • By Air: Nearest airport is Hyderabad (Rajiv Gandhi International Airport), roughly a 4–5 hour drive.

Nearby on the 50 KM Circuit

Triveni Sangamam, Adi Mukteshwara Temple, 108 Shiva Lingala Temple, Saraswati Temple, Medigadda & Annaram Barrages, and the temple clusters at Mahadevpur, Chennur and Manthani are all within easy reach. See the full interactive map on the 50 KM Circuit page.
