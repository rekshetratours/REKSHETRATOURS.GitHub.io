Rekshetra Tours

Kaleshwaram temple tourism specialists, based right in Kaleshwaram.

Who We Are

Rekshetra Tours plans pilgrimage visits to Sri Kaleshwara Mukteshwara Swamy Temple and the wider 50km circuit of temples, theerthas and heritage sites around Kaleshwaram, Telangana. Being based locally, we help with everything from a same-day darshan to a multi-day family pilgrimage — travel, stay assistance and a itinerary suited to your group.

Privacy Policy

We only use the details you send us (name, phone, dates, group size) to plan and confirm your trip. Nothing you submit through the Plan Your Trip form is stored on this website — it is sent directly to us by WhatsApp or email, whichever you choose. We do not sell or share your information with third parties.

Terms & Conditions

  • Tour packages and prices are confirmed only after direct communication with our team on WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • Temple darshan timings are set by the temple administration and may change without notice, especially on festival days.
  • Cancellations and rescheduling are handled case by case — please contact us as early as possible.
