Suggested tour options | యాత్రా ఎంపికలు

  • One-day visit: Suitable only when the departure time, road travel and temple schedule allow a practical same-day trip. Review the one-day itinerary.
  • Two-day pilgrimage: Consider this option if you want more time for temple visits, theerthalu and nearby sacred places. See the two-day pilgrimage plan.
  • Custom temple circuit: Select nearby temples based on your available time using the nearby temples guide.

Travel distances and times vary by route, traffic, road conditions and stops. Use Google Maps for the current route and confirm local access before departure. Tour pricing and vehicle availability must be confirmed before booking.
