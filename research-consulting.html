About the Researcher

Ramakant Eshwara Sharma P is a Ph.D. Research Scholar in Commerce at Nirwan University Jaipur, with over ten years of experience as an Outsourcing Junior Assistant at Sri Kaleshwara Muktheeshwara Swamy Temple, Kaleshwaram.

His research interests focus on temple economics, pilgrimage tourism, devotee services, financial management and rural livelihood development.

Research Project

Temple-Centric Sustainable Development Model

A Commerce-Based Approach for Revenue Generation, Devotee Services, and Rural Employment – Telangana.

The study aims to:

  • Examine temple revenue sources and management.
  • Study devotee service quality and accessibility.
  • Identify tourism-related local employment opportunities.
  • Assess opportunities for rural entrepreneurship.
  • Develop evidence-based recommendations for sustainable development.

Proposed Research & Consulting Services

  • Temple tourism research and documentation.
  • Devotee satisfaction surveys.
  • Revenue and financial data analysis.
  • Rural employment and livelihood studies.
  • Project reports and feasibility studies.
  • Excel-based research analysis and Power BI dashboards.

Services are subject to relevant qualifications, project requirements, applicable permissions and mutually agreed terms.
